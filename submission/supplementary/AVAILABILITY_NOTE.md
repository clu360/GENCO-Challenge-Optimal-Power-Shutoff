# Current availability and scope

This note accompanies the unchanged GOC-500 technical report. Its final-page
workshop checklist and statement that code/data will be provided “upon
acceptance” predate the GENCO Challenge artifact release. The current repository
README, data catalog, model manifests, and verification record supersede that
availability statement; they do not change the report's scientific results.

The repository includes retained GOC-500/Texas result tables, compact publication
data, runnable adapters and notebooks, and checksum-bound acquisition of 14
unique weight files exposed through 15 model catalog entries. Exact modified
Texas electrical inputs remain private. Public weather-derived exposure and a
small official IEEE14 example are included. The official source and all 750
fingerprints for the GOC held-out population are documented, but full population
recovery and historical OPS/AC recomputation have not been reverified here.

Repository/model access currently requires authorization. A public challenge
submission must use links verified without account access. The three-page entry
distinguishes completed GENCO experiments, the earlier GridSFM study, and the
proposed shortlist-based risk-aware AC refinement. The proposed refinement is
not part of the completed experiments.
